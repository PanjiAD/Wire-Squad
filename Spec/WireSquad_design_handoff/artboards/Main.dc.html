Wire Squad: design system (P0)
Original friendly bold-comic style for a private cooperative bomb-defusal deduction game. Thick ink outlines, flat offset shadows, halftone texture. System fonts and inline SVG only.

1. Swatch sheet

Functional colors are fixed. Each has tint, light, base and shade; contrast ratios are computed against ink and cream. Text on a swatch uses whichever of ink or cream passes 4.5:1.

2. Type scale

Display: Impact stack (stencil/comic feel, no external font). Body: system-ui. Wire numerals use the display face at the heaviest available weight, tabular. 6 and 9 get an underline wherever they are shown on a tile or token.
