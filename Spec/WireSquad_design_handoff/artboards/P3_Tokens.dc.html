P3: Tokens, markers and trays
Info tokens, = and ≠ tokens, markers, validation tokens, the two trays and their motion. All inline SVG, no external assets.

Flag/bookmark body with a notched bottom edge and a chevron at the top that points UP at the wire it describes. White numeral, 6 and 9 underlined. Yellow token shows a bold square. Two tokens exist per number (28 in total with the two yellow).

2. = and ≠ tokens

Rounded squares with a small notch at the top (a tiny "eye" bump). Blue = for equal, yellow ≠ for different. They sit between two neighbours, so each has a wide version spanning two tile widths.

= token (blue)
≠ token (yellow)
= wide: spans two tile widths, centering tick
≠ wide
Sits BETWEEN two neighbouring wires of the same stand.

3. Markers

Tiny stout cylinders seen from above, with a plain side and a "?" side. They flag which wire values are in play on the number track (the "?" side means "one of these may be real").

Translucent green glass disc with a highlight arc and a check mark. Sized to sit over a number on the track and cover it (cell-shaded glass).

5. Trays

Info tray: blue frame, 28 slots (two per number 1-12 plus two yellow), each labelled so a taken token leaves a visible ghost number. Validation tray: green frame, 12 slots. When the two tokens for a number are gone the UI shows a public text marker instead (FAQ).
