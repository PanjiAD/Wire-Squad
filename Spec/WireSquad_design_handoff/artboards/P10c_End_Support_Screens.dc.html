How to play

You may not say or hint at wire values outside the game actions.
TurnActionsWiresEquipmentCommunication rules
Dual Cut. Point at a teammate wire and name a value you hold. A match cuts both wires. A miss moves the dial.
Solo Cut. If every remaining wire of a value is in your hand, cut them all. No risk.
Reveal red. Only when all your uncut wires are red.

Mission 5 rules

MISSION RULES

Draw 3 yellow wires. Put 3 markers, ? side up, on those values on the track.

Shuffle the 3 yellow tiles face down. Add 2 to the blue pile; discard the third unseen.

The 2 yellow wires are cut together; their values are among the 3 marked.

FAILURE:The dial reaches the skull, or the red wire is cut.
SUCCESS:All wires cut.
