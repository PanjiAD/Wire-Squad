P5: Game board
Central board built from separate layers. Calm by design: the player attention goes to the stands and the dial.

1. Desktop board, 16:9 (1120 x 630)

Named SVG groups (ids on this first copy): #bb-board-bg, #bb-track, #bb-equip-slots, #bb-info-tray, #bb-valid-tray, #bb-dial-slot, #bb-mission-slot, #bb-captain-banner. Props on the desk are low-contrast so the eye goes to the stands and the dial. The demo shows slot 1 locked, slot 2 unlocked, slots 3 to 5 empty, slots 4 and 5 dimmed with a 4 / 5 player pip.

2. Layout wireframe, desktop

Exact pixel box for each layer in board coordinates (x, y, w x h), so real components drop into the slots.

4. Equipment slot states

Five slots, each with a small up-arrow at the left and right, a red cross at the top and a green check at the bottom. A card is slid up to reveal the check when it unlocks.
