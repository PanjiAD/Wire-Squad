P4: Detonator dial
A round gauge that shows how many mistakes the team has left. Needle, face and surround are separate layers so the needle can animate on its own.

1. Dial base (static) and needle group

Seven zones clockwise from the top: green (5 trainee helmets), yellow-green (4), yellow (3), orange (2), orange-red (empty), red with a skull, purple with a U-turn arrow and a warning triangle. Behind it a bundle of dynamite bound with white wire and a small timer. The needle is a SEPARATE group (class .bb-needle) with its hub as the pivot.

Helmet icon = original
A small helmeted trainee head, not an animal and not from any existing game. The count of icons is the start zone for the team size: 5 players start on the 5-icon zone, 4 on the 4-icon zone, 3 on 3, 2 on 2.

3. Explosion overlay and danger pulse

Explosion: shake (500ms), red/white flash, cracked-glass lines over the face. Danger pulse: a slow red glow every 1.6s, stops under reduced motion (the board then keeps a static red vignette and the icon-warning text "1 mistake from the skull").

4. Rotation angles for JS

Needle rests pointing at 12 o'clock when the angle is 0, so each zone center is (zone - 0.5) x 51.43 degrees. Zone 4 is exactly 180 deg (straight down).
