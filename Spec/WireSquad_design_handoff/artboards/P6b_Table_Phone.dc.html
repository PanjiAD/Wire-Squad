Phone rules

Strip: opponents collapse to a 100 to 170 px wide card with name, a mini rack of tile backs, and the shelf / cut strip as thin bars.

Expand: tapping a card opens a full-width rack (wire width 26 px) for choosing a target. A second tap on the wire pins it.

Own stand: fixed above the action bar; 14 tiles at 23 px fit a 360 px screen.

Action bar: four 48 px high buttons. Disabled actions stay visible but grey, so the player sees why they cannot use them.

Log: bottom sheet, collapsed to the last event.
