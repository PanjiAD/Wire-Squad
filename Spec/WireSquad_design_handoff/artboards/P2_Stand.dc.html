P2: Tile stand (rack) and player hand
Long dark rack with a front lip and narrow slots. Built in CSS + inline SVG tiles, driven by three variables: --bb-tile-w, --bb-tile-h, --bb-slot-gap.

1. Own stand and opponent stand

A: tiles face the viewer. B: tile backs from across the table, drawn with a real 9 degree perspective tilt in CSS. Rack width follows the tile width variable, so JS only sets --bb-tile-w.

B: opponent stand

2. Resizable: 4, 8, 12 and 14 slots

The longest rack (14) must fit a 360px phone: tile width 23px, side padding trimmed. Below 28px the UI switches the active stand to a zoom / focus view or scrolls horizontally (overflow-x:auto on .rack).

3. Two-stand hand

Some players hold two stands. They are stacked and joined by one yellow bracket and one shared nameplate, so the player reads it as ONE hand. A stand can be 1 to 14 slots.

5. Info shelf, cut strip, empty slots, cleared stand

The cleared state is dimmed and stamped. Empty slots are dashed outlines (used when a Walkie-Talkies swap removes a wire before the new one lands).

Info shelf: a low shelf in front of the stand; each token stands under its wire and carries a 4px alignment tick (the small line above the token). Cut strip: cut tiles lie flat, face up (P1 cut state).

A yellow pin hovers over a chosen target wire. Multi-select shows numbered badges (1, 2, 3) for the Double / Triple Detector. Whole-stand select (Super Detector) pins every wire and outlines the stand.
